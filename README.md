## LiveNow - live today

LiveNow App helps you decide what to do right now. You share who you are with, your energy, and how much time you have — we suggest one random activity from a local catalog. Enjoy your life outside device screens.

LiveNow is intended for adults (18+). Users under 18 should only use the app with a parent or guardian’s supervision,
Ideas are inspiration only — not medical, legal, financial, or professional advice. You are responsible for your safety and choices,
